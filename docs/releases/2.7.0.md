# APD Project Management Dashboard 2.7.0

**A more modern look, new ways to see projects, and dashboard widgets.**

Only the screens changed: no data, settings or database tables are touched.

## The look
- **GVW colours:** the accent is the water blue from the GVW logo, and dark mode leans towards GVW navy.
- **Inter font**, built into the app so it doesn't depend on Google. Numbers line up in tables.
- **Softer cards:** rounder corners, gentler shadows and lighter borders.
- **Icons instead of text links and emoji.**
  - Rows in every register have edit, duplicate and delete icons.
  - The projects table has a ⋯ menu on each row (Open, Edit, Delete).
- **Motion:**
  - Record forms slide in from the right as side panels, so the list stays visible behind them.
  - Menus pop in, loading placeholders shimmer, and status changes show straight away while they save.
  - All motion is turned off for anyone who has "reduce motion" set on their device.

## Top bar
- **Round avatar with your initials.** Its menu holds:
  - Light/dark mode
  - Table **density** (Comfortable or Compact)
  - Keyboard shortcuts
  - Password, two-factor sign-in and email digest
  - Task templates and Manage users
  - Log out
- **Search is a pill** with a Ctrl+K hint.
- Search and notification menus now close when you click anywhere else on the page.

## Controls
- **Date picker:** every date field opens a calendar in the app's own style, with Today and Clear. The monthly report sheet keeps its spreadsheet-style date cell.
- **Styled drop-downs**, and the dashboard's **status chips show counts**.
- **Empty lists show an icon, a short message and an Add button.**

## Dashboard
- **Stat tiles** have an icon and what changed recently. Total projects and Total approved budget also have a 12-month trend line with a hover readout.
- **Table / Board / Timeline** switch for projects (remembered):
  - **Board:** a column per status. Drag a card to change its status (projects you can edit only).
  - **Timeline:** every project from start to target, with a Today line and overdue projects marked.
- **Widgets** in the right-hand column:
  - **Portfolio health** (on track, watch, at risk)
  - **Due soon**
  - A **calendar** with dots on due days; click a day to see what's due
  - **Next completions** with a countdown
  - Quick links
- **Spend chart:** a shaded area under actual spend, and a hover crosshair that reads out each month against the budget.

## Project page
- **At a glance** now shows:
  - **Time elapsed** and **Budget spent** meters. Budget turns amber when spending runs well ahead of time, and red past the budget.
  - A **key-dates line**: start, target, revised and actual, with Today marked.
- A row of **open items** (risks, actions, defects, RFIs: click one to jump to its list) and the **next milestone** with a countdown.
- The phone bottom bar uses icons.

## Update

On the server: `git pull`, then `docker compose up -d --build`. The left menu should then show v2.7.0. Reload the page once so the new font and styles load.
