# APD Project Management Dashboard 3.0.0

**Approvals, capital works by financial year, comments, change history, cost forecasting and more.**

A new section version: Approvals and Capital works are new parts of the app. Nothing existing is changed or deleted. The update adds three database tables (approval requests, field changes, comments) and a few optional fields. The migration runs by itself when the backend starts.

## New sections
- **Approvals** (menu → Approvals, G P):
  - Send a variation or payment claim to a named person with **Request approval** on its row.
  - They approve or reject it with a comment; rejecting needs a reason.
  - Every request and decision is kept, and the row shows "Awaiting…", "Approved by…" or "Rejected by…".
  - Approving a variation sets it to Approved, and rejecting sets it to Rejected.
  - Waiting approvals appear in the approver's notifications and email digest, with a count in the menu.
- **Capital works by financial year** (menu → Capital works, G F):
  - Each project's planned (forecast) and actual spend per Australian financial year (July to June), with totals and a chart.
  - Filter by project manager, and export to CSV.

## Projects
- **Summary PDF:** a one-page "project on a page" for steering committees and sponsors, covering:
  - status and health
  - time and budget bars
  - key dates
  - top risks
  - next milestones
  - recent decisions
- **Cost forecast** on Budget & Contract:
  - forecast final cost (spend to date plus remaining forecast) and the variance to budget
  - monthly burn rate and the months of budget left at that rate
  - contingency used by approved variations (contingency comes from the new project field, or else from its contracts)
- **Risk heat map:** a 5×5 likelihood-by-consequence grid, inherent or residual, on each project and across the portfolio (Alerts). Click a cell to list its risks.
- **Change history** (History button): who changed what, when, with old and new values, for project details, variations, payment claims and risks. Recorded from this version on.
- **Comments with @mentions** on risks, RFIs, actions, variations, defects and payment claims. The mentioned person is notified.
- **Templates by asset type:** task templates can carry an asset type and milestones. A new project offers the matching template and sets up its tasks and milestones.
- **Board view** for tasks and actions. Drag cards between columns, or use the arrow keys.

## Dashboard and reporting
- **Missing details** widget: projects without a project manager, dates, TRIM folder, budget or this month's report.
- **Customise** the right-hand widgets: show, hide and reorder them.
- **Since your last visit:** what others changed on your projects.
- **Notifications:**
  - Now include mentions, approvals and report reminders.
  - Read/unread, snooze for a day or a week, and Mark all read, saved on your account.
- **Monthly reports:**
  - Reports page shows who has started this month's report.
  - Admins can **draft every missing report** in one go.
  - Project managers are reminded from the 22nd, in the app and the digest.
  - **Save PDF to SharePoint** puts a report's PDF in `_PDFs` beside the linked workbook.

## Speed and accessibility
- Responses are compressed by the backend: a projects list goes from 5.5 KB to 1.3 KB.
- A "Skip to content" link, keyboard-openable project rows and cards, and arrow keys in menus.
- Labelled controls, and higher-contrast muted text. An automated WCAG 2 AA scan (axe) of the dashboard, a project page, Approvals and Capital works reports no serious or critical issues.

## Update
